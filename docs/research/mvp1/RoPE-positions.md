# RoPE positions on a frame smaller than the trained one

Consider the RoPE positions for height and width when the frame is reduced from the trained 704×1280 to 512×928.

$$
\underbrace{\mathrm{scaleLen}=\frac{L'}{L}}_{\text{new length to trained length}},\qquad
\underbrace{\mathrm{scaleLen}_{\rm H}=\frac{512/32}{704/32}=\frac{16}{22}\approx0.73}_{\text{we have 16 of 22 tokens left in height}},\qquad
\underbrace{\theta_d=b^{-2d/\lvert D\rvert}}_{\text{RoPE frequencies}}
$$

All three papers below solve the inverse problem, $\mathrm{scaleLen}>1$: extending the length. None of them considers $\mathrm{scaleLen}<1$; PI and
YaRN take only $L'>L$, dynamic YaRN restricts $\mathrm{scaleLen}=\max(1,l'/L)$, RIFLEx considers a small extension
solved without changes. The transfer to $\mathrm{scaleLen}<1$ below is our hypothesis.
