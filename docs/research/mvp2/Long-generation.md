# Long generation

**Live 3 (LongLive) and earlier Wan / Self-Forcing experiments**

How to continue a video with limited memory? Consider the KV of one block.

$$
\underbrace{n}_{\text{number of finished chunks}},\quad\underbrace{\mathrm{lat}_n}_{\text{finished chunk}},\quad
\underbrace{\mathrm{KVvid}_n=(K_n,V_n)}_{\text{saved video memory}},\quad
\underbrace{\mathrm{ctx}}_{\text{unchanged text condition}}
$$
$$
(\mathrm{KVvid}_n,\mathrm{ctx},\underbrace{\epsilon_{n+1}}_{\text{new noise}})
\xrightarrow{\text{generation of the next chunk}}
(\mathrm{lat}_{n+1},\mathrm{KVvid}_{n+1}),\qquad n=0,1,\ldots
$$

Now consider the structure of the memory: which K/V we save for the next step. Let us split it into a constant anchor and an updated part.

$$
\underbrace{\oplus}_{\text{concatenation of K and V rows}},\qquad
\underbrace{\mathrm{KVvid}_n=\mathrm{KVanch}\oplus\mathrm{KVupd}_n}_{\text{memory before generating chunk }n+1},\qquad
\underbrace{\mathrm{KVanch}}_{\text{K/V of the initial frames}},\quad
\underbrace{\mathrm{KVupd}_n}_{\text{updated part of K/V}}
$$
$$
\lvert\mathrm{KVvid}_n\rvert
  =\lvert\mathrm{KVanch}\rvert+\lvert\mathrm{KVupd}_n\rvert
  =F_{\rm saved,n}\,N_{\rm frame}.
$$
$$
\underbrace{\mathrm{KVnew}_{n+1}=(K_{\rm new},V_{\rm new})}_{\text{K/V of the new chunk}},\qquad

\underbrace{N_{\rm max}}_{\text{maximum tokens in attention}}
$$

	Φ denotes the memory update rule; n → n+1 means the transition to the next chunk.

**Live 3: ring KV cache**

In a full Live 3 window, buffer slots 0-7 hold the anchor, 8-23 hold the ring, and 24-31 hold the incoming chunk. These are buffer slots. The Rust runtime indexes the incoming chunk's temporal RoPE by its position in the video plus the scene offset.

FIFO (First In, First Out) changes only $\mathrm{KVupd}_n$. The anchor $\mathrm{KVanch}$ stays the same; the buffer reuses the chosen places in $\mathrm{KVupd}_n$.

$$
\underbrace{\mathrm{IDX}_{\rm evict}}_{\text{indices of evicted rows}}:
\text{the oldest unpinned rows in the ring},
\qquad
\mathrm{IDX}_{\rm evict}\subseteq\mathrm{rows}(\mathrm{KVupd}_n),\qquad
\mathrm{KVupd}_{n+1}[\mathrm{IDX}_{\rm evict}]\leftarrow\mathrm{KVnew}_{n+1},\qquad
\mathrm{KVvid}_{n+1}=\mathrm{KVanch}\oplus\mathrm{KVupd}_{n+1}.
$$

The ring reuses a fixed buffer, replacing the oldest unpinned rows.
